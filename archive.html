about

this website archives tumblr images collected during 2014-about 2023 (ultimately dissolving with the tumblr ban and covid). this archive of images and gifs was collected and bounced back and forth between myself and an online group of artist friends. the hyperimages/hyperculture shaped our aesthetic tastes through this amalgamation. we never returned to collecting images at this scale, most of us have not kept a collection on tumblr since covid, and we have all drifted off on our own separate paths. but i still remember, i will safekeep it forever. **disclaimer** these images have been web scraped from tumblr blogs and I do not claim ownership of any of the images.

created for sfpc digital decay spring 2026 class! link

Ghost entries are represented by blank spaces in my data collection. It also represents the many online friends I don't have contact with anymore and cannot be found. Metadata is both found and manufactured by emotion and memory.
