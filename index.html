tumblr archive 2014-2023

about

this website archives tumblr images collected during 2014-about 2023 (ultimately dissolving with the tumblr ban and covid). this archive of images and gifs was collected and bounced back and forth between myself and an online group of artist friends. the hyperimages/hyperculture shaped our aesthetic tastes through this amalgamation. we never returned to collecting images at this scale, most of us have not kept a collection on tumblr since covid, and we have all drifted off on our own separate paths. but i still remember, i will safekeep it forever. **disclaimer** these images have been web scraped from tumblr blogs and I do not claim ownership of any of the images. link
